Our Roots: The Metro Vitals Pharmacy

Before we expanded into a full-service medical clinic, Metro Vitals started as a trusted neighborhood pharmacy. Today, that same pharmacy is the heartbeat of our facility.

Skip the extra trip across town. Walk out of your doctor's appointment and step right up to our pharmacy counter to have your prescriptions filled on the spot.

  • Prescription Fulfillment Fast, accurate dispensing directly after your consultation.
  • Over-The-Counter Essentials A curated selection of wellness products, vitamins, and first-aid supplies.
  • Expert Consultations Direct access to our head pharmacists for medication management and advice.

Ready to prioritize your health?
